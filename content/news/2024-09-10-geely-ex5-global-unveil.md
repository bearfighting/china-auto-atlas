Geely Auto unveiled the EX5 at Automechanika Frankfurt in September 2024 as a global electric SUV. The company described the model as being based on the GEA architecture and highlighted its global-market development approach.

The announcement also connected the EX5 with Geely's Short Blade Battery and 11-in-1 electric drive. The article preserves these as manufacturer-stated technology applications; comparative terms such as “class-leading” are not treated as independently verified facts.
