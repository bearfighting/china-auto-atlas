AVATR Technology launched the CHN All New Smart Electric Vehicle Architecture on June 25, 2022, together with Changan Automobile, Huawei and CATL. The launch identified AVATR 11 as the first model on the architecture.

The source describes different contributions from the three organizations, including complete-vehicle development, intelligent-vehicle solutions, batteries, motor drive, electronic control, energy management and charging infrastructure. These are recorded as sourced collaboration claims rather than as a single ownership relationship.
