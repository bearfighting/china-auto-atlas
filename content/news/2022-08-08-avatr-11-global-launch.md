AVATR Technology launched the AVATR 11 and the limited AVATR 011 edition on August 8, 2022. The launch included China pricing, configuration information and pre-order benefits.

AVATR described the vehicle as the first product built on the CHN technology platform. The announcement also associated the vehicle with CATL battery packs, Huawei DriveOne electric drive and the platform's high-voltage charging system. Configuration and performance statements remain claims tied to the launch source and should not be generalized to every later model year.
