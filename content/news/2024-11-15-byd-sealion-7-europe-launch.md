BYD introduced the SEALION 7 to the European market in November 2024. The manufacturer describes the model as using its Blade Battery and e-Platform 3.0, with Cell-to-Body integration presented as part of the vehicle architecture.

Range, power and charging figures remain manufacturer-reported specifications for the listed market variants.
